Create the key

Apple only lets you download this file on a computer, which is why your phone sent you here.

  1. Sign in at appstoreconnect.apple.com, then go to Users and Access > Integrations > Team Keys.
  2. Click the + button. Name the key Panvio, set Access to App Manager, then click Generate.
  3. Click Download next to the new key. Apple gives you this file once.
  4. Copy the Issuer ID from the top of the page.
Drop the .p8 here or click to choose it

Your key goes straight to Panvio and is encrypted there. This page keeps no copy and stores nothing in your browser.
